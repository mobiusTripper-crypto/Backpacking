03 Seasickness
toneNobody's too tough. Trade swell gets people. No judgment — say something early.
⚠ say somethingFeel off in any way — queasy, headache, just not right — tell me immediately.
medsDramamine works only if taken before. Night before or 1hr before underway. Too late once sick.
patchScopolamine = most effective. Rx required. Behind the ear the night before, not morning of.
braceletsSea-Bands — I have an extra to borrow. On before you feel sick. Ask before we leave the dock.
gingerGinger chews or ginger ale. Actually works for some. Zero side effects.
doctorsMedical professionals on all three boats. I'm not a doctor. Ask them.
⚠ stay on deckGoing below makes it worse. No screens or reading below if you're queasy.
horizonFixed point, far away. It works.
take the helmAsk me. Task + watching ahead helps more than almost anything.
midshipCenter moves least. Avoid the bow in rough conditions.
if sickLeeward (downwind) side — I'll show you. Small sips of water. Saltines. It passes.
04 Moving Around
ALWAYS: One hand for you, one hand for the boat.
underwayEverything moves more than you expect. Slow down.
aft doorAlways closed underway.
foredeckStay aft in 3+ ft waves.
nightRed lights preferred — white kills night vision.
05 Engines & Props — The Big One
DANGER: Props are 2–3 ft from the swim platforms. Helm is blind there.
⚠ the ruleNobody in the water if engines are on. Check with me before swimming.
returnClear the platform, THEN I start engines.
⚠ CO riskEngine & generator exhaust pools at the surface near the stern. Same rule.
fouled propStop engines. DO NOT REVERSE. Find me.
06 Propane
DANGER: Heavier than air. Pools in bilge. Single spark = explosion.
cookTank open → solenoid ON → light immediately.
doneSolenoid OFF → burn off → tank closed.
⚠ smellRotten egg: evacuate, NO switches, find me.
noteConfirm solenoid at check-in. Walk them through before solo use.
