Route Highlights

The trip began with a significant descent of nearly 6,000 feet on the first day, dropping from the rim down into the canyon. The middle days involved traversing through the canyon with moderate elevation changes. The final day featured the most challenging climb, gaining 6,654 feet to exit the canyon.

The balanced elevation gain and loss (20,371 ft vs 20,051 ft) indicates a loop or rim-to-rim route, returning to a similar elevation as the starting point.
